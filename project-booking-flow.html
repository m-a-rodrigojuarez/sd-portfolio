City Express · Product Strategy

Building a distribution platform that could scale

Redesigning the Channel Manager to make new integrations faster, reduce operational friction, and support new business models.

01

The challenge

Growth was exposing the limits of the architecture.

Each new OTA brought its own development and maintenance burden to a small technology team.

02

Understanding the problem

The problem wasn't just integration speed.

Technical reviews and Call Center data showed that the same complexity was affecting both development and hotel operations.

I built an error catalog from Call Center reports to identify and measure recurring failures.

03

The strategic move

Separate what changes from what should stay common.

We redesigned the architecture around reusable components, isolating partner-specific rules from the processing shared across integrations.

04

Designing for operations

A failed reservation shouldn't become a manual workflow.

We redesigned error handling so teams could fix the underlying issue and reprocess the reservation instead of rebuilding it manually across multiple systems.

05

Results and what it enabled

Less complexity. More room to grow.

~4 weeks New OTA integration vs. nearly 2 months
-90% Manual correction time
+38% Reservations
+19% Revenue
“

A more scalable platform made integrations easier to build, reservations easier to recover, and new business opportunities easier to pursue.
