About me

Researcher by practice, product and technology by background.

Hi, I'm Rodrigo, a Product & UX Researcher based in Ensenada, Baja California. My background spans research, product management, and software development, so I tend to look beyond the user experience to understand how people, operations, business needs, and technology come together — especially when the problem isn't clearly defined yet.

Most recently at Walmart Mexico, I led research across eCommerce and transportation products, working with Product, Design, Operations, and business teams on problems ranging from onboarding and last-mile operations to store cash logistics. Before that, I worked in UX consulting at Sperientia and in product and technology at Hoteles City Express.

I also teach quantitative UX methods at Universidad ICESI and advise graduate research. Teaching keeps me close to evidence and measurement, while my engineering background keeps me asking whether an idea can actually work in the real world.

Outside work, you'll usually find me playing football, training CrossFit, or enjoying life in Ensenada.

How I work

Start with the problem, not the method.

I don't start with a favorite framework or research method. I start by understanding the decision we need to make, what we don't know yet, and what evidence would help us move forward.

Frame complex problems

I look beyond the immediate user experience to understand the workflows, constraints, stakeholders, and business context shaping the problem.

Build the right evidence

I choose qualitative, quantitative, behavioral, or operational evidence based on the question, and combine them when one source isn't enough.

Turn evidence into direction

I bring teams into the process to make sense of what we learn, explore options, and turn the evidence into priorities, requirements, or product decisions.
